# Research Protocol — Pilot Study

## Working Title
Climate Blind Spots in the Forensic Microbial Clock: A Pilot Evidence Map

## Research Question
How consistently do published studies investigating postmortem microbial changes report environmental conditions and sampling information?

## Research Objectives
1. Identify the geographical settings represented in selected postmortem microbiome studies.
2. Examine whether studies report environmental temperature and humidity.
3. Compare reported sampling locations and microbiological methods.
4. Identify methodological reporting gaps that may affect comparisons between studies.

## Study Design
Pilot structured evidence map using published peer-reviewed scientific studies.

## Planned Sample
Approximately 12–15 original studies published between 2015 and 2026.

## Preliminary Eligibility Criteria

Include:
- Original research involving postmortem microbial communities and postmortem interval.
- Human or animal decomposition studies.
- Studies with sufficient accessible methodological information for screening.

Exclude:
- Literature reviews and opinion articles.
- Studies unrelated to postmortem interval.
- Articles without relevant microbial observations.

## Planned Analysis
Extract study characteristics into a spreadsheet. Calculate descriptive counts and percentages. Record missing information as "Not reported."

## Current Status
Planning stage. Research question and methods are subject to refinement following a broader literature search.
